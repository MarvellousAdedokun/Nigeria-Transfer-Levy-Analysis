                                            KEY HIGHLIGHTS

1. The Federation Account Allocation Committee (FAAC) disbursed the sum of 3.40 trillion to the
three tiers of government in June 2026 from the total revenue generated in May 2026.
2. The amount disbursed comprised:
· 2.65 trillion recorded from the Statutory Account, and
· 743.67 billion from Value Added Tax.
3. The allocations to the three tiers of government for June 2026 were:
· Federal Government with a total of 818.68 billion
· States with a total of 759.14 billion, and
· Local Governments with a total of 534.28 billion.
4. The sum of 188.13 billion was shared among the oil-producing states from the 13% derivation
fund.
5. The revenue generating agencies comprising Nigeria Revenue Service (NRS), and Nigerian Up-

stream Petroleum Regulatory Commission (NUPRC) received 84.69 billion and 38.85 billion, re-

spectively, as cost of revenue collections.

                                                                       4
6. Further breakdown of revenue allocation distribution to the Federal Government of Ni-
geria (FGN) revealed that:
· 651.13 billion was disbursed to the FGN consolidated revenue account,
· 14.23 billion was received as a share of derivation and ecology,
· 7.12 billion as stabilization fund,
· 23.91 billion for the development of natural resources, and
· 30.62 billion to the Federal Capital Territory (FCT)- Abuja.
