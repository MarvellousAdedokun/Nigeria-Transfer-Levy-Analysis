                                            KEY HIGHLIGHTS

1. The Federation Account Allocation Committee (FAAC) disbursed the sum of 2.85 trillion to the
three tiers of government in May 2025 from the total revenue generated in April 2025
2. The amount disbursed comprised:
· 2.08 trillion recorded from the Statutory Account,
· 81.41 billion from Exchange Gain,
· 40.48billion from Electronic Money Transfer Levy (EMTL), and
· 642.26 billion from Value Added Tax.
3. The allocations to the three tiers of government for January 2025 were:
· Federal Government with a total of 565.31 billion
· States with a total of 556.74 billion, and
· Local Governments with a total of 406.63 billion.
4. The sum of 152.55 billion was shared among the oil-producing states from the 13% derivation
fund.
5. The revenue generating agencies comprising Nigeria Customs Service (NCS), Federal Inland Rev-
enue Service (FIRS), and Nigerian Upstream Petroleum Regulatory Commission (NUPRC) received
28.62 billion, 46.28 billion, and 26.15 billion respectively, as cost of revenue collections.

                                                                       4
6. Further breakdown of revenue allocation distribution to the Federal Government of Ni-
geria (FGN) revealed that:
· 415.08 billion was disbursed to the FGN consolidated revenue account,
· 8.92 billion was received as a share of derivation and ecology,
· 4.46 billion as stabilization fund,
· 14.98 billion for the development of natural resources, and
· 14.91 billion to the Federal Capital Territory (FCT)- Abuja.
