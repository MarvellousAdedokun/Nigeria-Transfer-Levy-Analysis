The Federation Account Allocation Committee (FAAC) data is provided statutorily by the Of-
fice of the Accountant General of the Federation (OAGF), and reported by the National Bu-
reau of Statistics.

                                            KEY HIGHLIGHTS

1. The Federation Account Allocation Committee (FAAC) disbursed the sum of 2.59 trillion to the

three tiers of government in January 2026 from the total revenue generated in December 2025
2. The amount disbursed comprised:

· 1.63 trillion recorded from the Statutory Account,
· 39.91 billion from Electronic Money Transfer Levy (EMTL), and
· 913.96 billion from Value Added Tax.

3. The allocations to the three tiers of government for January 2026 were:

· Federal Government with a total of 653.50 billion
· States with a total of 706.47 billion, and
· Local Governments with a total of 513.27 billion.
4. The sum of 96.08 billion was shared among the oil-producing states from the 13% derivation

fund.
5. The revenue generating agencies comprising Nigeria Customs Service (NCS), Federal Inland Rev-
enue Service (FIRS), and Nigerian Upstream Petroleum Regulatory Commission (NUPRC) received

27.85 billion, 53.12 billion, and 23.72 billion respectively, as cost of revenue collections.

                                                                       4
6. Further breakdown of revenue allocation distribution to the Federal Government of Ni-
geria (FGN) revealed that:

· 496.33 billion was disbursed to the FGN consolidated revenue account,
· 9.89 billion was received as a share of derivation and ecology,
· 4.94 billion as stabilization fund,
· 16.61 billion for the development of natural resources, and
· 18.37 billion to the Federal Capital Territory (FCT)- Abuja.
