                                             METHODOLOGY

The Federation Account Allocation Committee (FAAC) data is provided statutorily by the Of-
fice of the Accountant General of the Federation (OAGF), and reported by the National Bu-
reau of Statistics.

                                            KEY HIGHLIGHTS

1. The Federation Account Allocation Committee (FAAC) disbursed the sum of 2.23 trillion to the

three tiers of government in March 2026 from the total revenue generated in February 2026
2. The amount disbursed comprised:

· 1.56 trillion recorded from the Statutory Account,
· 668.45 billion from Value Added Tax.

3. The allocations to the three tiers of government for March 2026 were:

· Federal Government with a total of 675.09 billion
· States with a total of 651.53 billion, and
· Local Governments with a total of 456.47 billion.
4. The sum of 110.95 billion was shared among the oil-producing states from the 13% derivation

fund.
5. The revenue generating agencies comprising Nigeria Revenue Service (NRS), and Nigerian Up-

stream Petroleum Regulatory Commission (NUPRC) received 53.48 billion and 23.83 billion, re-

spectively, as cost of revenue collections.

                                                                       4
6. Further breakdown of revenue allocation distribution to the Federal Government of Ni-
geria (FGN) revealed that:

· 519.01 billion was disbursed to the FGN consolidated revenue account,
· 11.64 billion was received as a share of derivation and ecology,
· 5.82 billion as stabilization fund,
· 19.55 billion for the development of natural resources, and
· 11.42 billion to the Federal Capital Territory (FCT)- Abuja.
