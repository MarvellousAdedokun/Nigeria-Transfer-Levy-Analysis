The Federation Account Allocation Committee (FAAC) data is provided statutorily by the Of-
fice of the Accountant General of the Federation (OAGF), and reported by the National Bu-
reau of Statistics.

                                            KEY HIGHLIGHTS

1. The Federation Account Allocation Committee (FAAC) disbursed the sum of 2.63 trillion in April

2026 from the total revenue generated in March 2026.
2. The amount disbursed comprised:

· 1.70 trillion recorded from the Statutory Account
· 200.00 billion from Non-Oil revenue
· 68.10 billion from Solid Minerals revenue
· 664.42 billion from Value Added Tax

3. The allocations to the three tiers of government for April 2026 were:

· Federal Government with a total of 820.37 billion
· States with a total of 673.43 billion, and
· Local Governments with a total of 481.03 billion.
4. The sum of 129.61 billion was shared among the oil-producing states from the 13% derivation

fund.
5. The revenue generating agencies comprising Nigeria Revenue Service (NRS), and Nigerian Up-

Stream Petroleum Regulatory Commission (NUPRC) received 57.52 billion and 23.56 billion, re-

spectively, as cost of revenue collections.

                                                                       4
6. Further breakdown of revenue allocation distribution to the Federal Government of Ni-
geria (FGN) revealed that:

· 651.85 billion was disbursed to the FGN Consolidated Revenue Fund Account
· 14.59 billion was allocated as a share of derivation and ecology
· 7.30 billion as stabilization fund
· 24.52 billion for the development of natural resources, and
· 23.21 billion to the Federal Capital Territory (FCT)- Abuja.
