                                             METHODOLOGY

The Federation Account Allocation Committee (FAAC) data is provided statutorily by the Of-
fice of the Accountant General of the Federation (OAGF), and reported by the National Bu-
reau of Statistics

                                                                       3
                                            KEY HIGHLIGHTS

1. The Federation Account Allocation Committee (FAAC) disbursed the sum of 2.27 trillion to the
three tiers of government in September 2024 from the total revenue generated in August 2024.
2. The amount disbursed comprised:
· 1.22 trillion recorded from the Statutory Account,
· 468.24 billion from Exchange Gain,
· 15.64 billion from Electronic Money Transfer Levy (EMTL), and
· 573.34 billion from Value Added Tax.
3. The allocations to the three tiers of government for September 2024 were:
· Federal Government with a total of 374.92 billion
· States with a total of 422.86 billion, and
· Local Governments with a total of 306.53 billion.
4. The sum of 99.47 billion was shared among the oil-producing states from the 13% derivation
fund.
5. The revenue generating agencies comprising Nigeria Customs Service (NCS), Federal Inland Rev-
enue Service (FIRS), and Nigerian Upstream Petroleum Regulatory Commission (NUPRC) received
21.73 billion, 45.07 billion and 15.16 billion respectively, as cost of revenue collections.

                                                                       4
6. Further breakdown of revenue allocation distribution to the Federal Government of Ni-
geria (FGN) revealed that:
· 241.29 billion was disbursed to the FGN consolidated revenue account,
· 5.55 billion was received as a share of derivation and ecology,
· 2.77 billion as stabilization fund,
· 9.33 billion for the development of natural resources, and
· 10.53 billion to the Federal Capital Territory (FCT)- Abuja.
