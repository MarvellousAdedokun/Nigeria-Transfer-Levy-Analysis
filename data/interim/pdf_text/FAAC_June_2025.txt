                                            KEY HIGHLIGHTS

1. The Federation Account Allocation Committee (FAAC) disbursed the sum of 2.94 trillion to the
three tiers of government in June 2025 from the total revenue generated in May 2025
2. The amount disbursed comprised:
· 2.09 trillion recorded from the Statutory Account,
· 76.61 billion from Exchange Gain,
· 28.82 billion from Electronic Money Transfer Levy (EMTL), and
· 742.82 billion from Value Added Tax.
3. The allocations to the three tiers of government for June 2025 were:
· Federal Government with a total of 538.00 billion
· States with a total of 577.84 billion, and
· Local Governments with a total of 419.97 billion.
4. The sum of 124.08 billion was shared among the oil-producing states from the 13% derivation
fund.
5. The revenue generating agencies comprising Nigeria Customs Service (NCS), Federal Inland Rev-
enue Service (FIRS), and Nigerian Upstream Petroleum Regulatory Commission (NUPRC) received
28.71 billion, 58.59 billion, and 24.61 billion respectively, as cost of revenue collections.

6. Further breakdown of revenue allocation distribution to the Federal Government of Ni-
geria (FGN) revealed that:
· 390.27 billion was disbursed to the FGN consolidated revenue account,
· 8.16 billion was received as a share of derivation and ecology,
· 4.08 billion as stabilization fund,
· 13.72 billion for the development of natural resources, and
· 14.97 billion to the Federal Capital Territory (FCT)- Abuja.
