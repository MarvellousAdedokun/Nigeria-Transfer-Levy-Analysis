The Federation Account Allocation Committee (FAAC) data is provided statutorily by the Of-

                                            KEY HIGHLIGHTS

1. The Federation Account Allocation Committee (FAAC) disbursed the sum of 4.50 trillion in July

2026 from the total revenue generated in June 2026.
2. The amount disbursed comprised:

· 3.45 trillion recorded from the Statutory Account,
· 799.75 billion from Value Added Tax., and
· 250.00 billion augmented funds.

3. The allocations to the three tiers of government for July 2026 were:

· Federal Government with a total of 1.06 trillion
· States with a total of 905.01 billion, and
· Local Governments with a total of 642.89 billion.
4. The sum of 197.61 billion was shared among the oil-producing states from the 13% derivation

fund.
5. The revenue generating agencies comprising Nigeria Revenue Service (NRS), and Nigerian Up-

stream Petroleum Regulatory Commission (NUPRC) received 108.83 billion and 51.91 billion,

respectively, as cost of revenue collections.

                                                                       4
6. Further breakdown of revenue allocation distribution to the Federal Government of Ni-
geria (FGN) revealed that:

· 868.78 billion was disbursed to the FGN consolidated revenue account,
· 18.62 billion was received as a share of derivation and ecology,
· 9.31 billion as stabilization fund,
· 31.29 billion for the development of natural resources, and
· 27.94 billion to the Federal Capital Territory (FCT)- Abuja.
