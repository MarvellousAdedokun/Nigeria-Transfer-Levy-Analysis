The Federation Account Allocation Committee (FAAC) data is provided statutorily by the Of-
fice of the Accountant General of the Federation (OAGF), and reported by the National Bu-
reau of Statistics.

                                            KEY HIGHLIGHTS

1. The Federation Account Allocation Committee (FAAC) disbursed the sum of 3.04 trillion to the

three tiers of government in February 2026 from the total revenue generated in January 2026
2. The amount disbursed comprised:

· 1.86 trillion recorded from the Statutory Account,
· 100 billion from Non-Oil Revenue, and
· 1.08 trillion from Value Added Tax.

3. The allocations to the three tiers of government for February 2026 were:

· Federal Government with a total of 577.91 billion
· States with a total of 794.01 billion, and
· Local Governments with a total of 537.88 billion.
4. The sum of 90.19 billion was shared among the oil-producing states from the 13% derivation

fund.
5. The revenue generating agencies comprising Federal Inland Revenue Service (FIRS), and Nigerian

Upstream Petroleum Regulatory Commission (NUPRC) received 87.49 billion and 21.44 billion,

respectively, as cost of revenue collections.

                                                                       4
6. Further breakdown of revenue allocation distribution to the Federal Government of Ni-
geria (FGN) revealed that:

· 433.46 billion was disbursed to the FGN consolidated revenue account,
· 9..07 billion was received as a share of derivation and ecology,
· 4.53 billion as stabilization fund,
· 15.23 billion for the development of natural resources, and
· 8.86 billion to the Federal Capital Territory (FCT)- Abuja.
